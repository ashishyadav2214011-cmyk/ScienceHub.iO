# AI Architecture

AI Router routes requests to authorized specialized AI.

Sukoon.Brain: live user chat. Hikaitage: learning and strategy. KuroVen: normal execution/action curation. World AI: technology/world information. HukoVaige: psychology, behaviour, mindset and personal development. Upgrade Guard AI: system/upgrade protection.

No AI may silently expand its authority. Hard privacy/security boundaries cannot be bypassed.
