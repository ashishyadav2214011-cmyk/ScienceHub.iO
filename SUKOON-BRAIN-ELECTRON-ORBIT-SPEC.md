# Sukoon.Brain — 8 Electron Orbit Specification

Exactly 8 visible electrons.

Each electron has an independent orbit/path and continuous realtime revolution.

Fixed revolution sequence:
1 → 2 seconds
2 → 3 seconds
3 → 4 seconds
4 → 5 seconds
5 → 6 seconds
6 → 7 seconds
7 → 8 seconds
8 → 9 seconds

This 2–9 second sequence is fixed. Electrons do not share one common revolution time and do not use a shared orbit.
